About this site
Who is this for? Undergraduate and master's students who already use, or are thinking about using, generative AI tools for study and want to do it responsibly.

AI-Smart Study is an independent student project for educational use. It gives general guidance only and is not official university policy or legal advice. Always follow your own course and institution rules.
