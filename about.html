About & Feedback

What this site is for, what it is not, and how your data is handled.

Purpose

AI-Smart Study helps university students use generative AI tools responsibly, effectively and transparently. It was created as a student project for a Social Media Analytics course.

What this site does not do

  • It does not run AI models, create accounts or collect your name.
  • It does not give official policy, legal or academic-integrity rulings for any university.
  • It does not help anyone avoid course rules.

Privacy & analytics

This site is static, so the prompt builder, quiz and checklist all run in your browser. Prompt text, quiz answers and checklist ticks are not sent to a server by the site. Checklist ticks and your analytics choice are stored in your browser only.

If analytics is enabled, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and learn which pages and tools are used (for example how many people copy a prompt or finish the quiz). A banner asks for your choice, and you can change it later from the footer. Google handles this data under its own terms.
